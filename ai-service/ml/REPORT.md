# Lead propensity model: report

Generated by `ml/train_propensity.py` (seed 42). Do not edit by hand: re-run the script.

## What it is

A logistic regression that estimates how likely a new lead is to activate, trained on 5,000 leads from April-June 2026 (368 activations). Variant (b): only fields the advisor or a sales form can collect. It is linear on purpose: every score can be explained, and Java scores it without Python.

**What it does not tell you:** it ranks who is likely to activate, not who activates *because* you called. Finding 40% of activations in the top 20% is not 40% more sales.

## Decisions

- **Language left out.** With it, June AUC is 0.719 rather than 0.714, within noise, but only 13.4% of regional-language leads reach HOT, against 23.1% for Hindi. Without it: 22.2% and 17.9%. The lower rate may reflect how the old process served them, and a score shouldn't lock that in.
- **Live leads count as "Website form".** It's what the data supports, and it means live chats rarely reach HOT. Fields the advisor doesn't collect fall back to the reference level.
- **Leakage:** anything known only after the sales process is banned, and the script fails if it's used. See `LEAKY_COLUMNS` in the script.
